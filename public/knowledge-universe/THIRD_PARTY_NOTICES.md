## Three.js 0.187.0

- Project: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js
- Package: https://www.npmjs.com/package/three/v/0.187.0
- License: MIT, preserved in vendor/three.LICENSE and the standalone HTML.
- Usage: WebGL shader point volumes for luminous gas, stars, dark dust and relationship filaments; perspective camera, invisible sphere hit targets and raycasting.
